Zoom

Ctrl + scroll wheel, or trackpad pinchZoom where the mouse is
+ / - / 0Zoom in / zoom out / back to full page
Pinch or double-tap (touchscreen)Zoom in and out
Drag, or scroll, while zoomedMove around the page
Clicks on the sides while zoomedDon't turn the page, so you can't flip away from a panel by accident
Turning the pageGoes back to full page

Issues

N or Next issueOpen the next issue in the series
Next page on the last pageAlso opens the next issue
Previous page twice on the first pageOpens the previous issue at its last page
Esc or LibraryBack to your shelf

View

D or layout buttonAuto, one page, or two pages. Auto shows two on wide screens, one on tall ones
W or Fit widthOne page across the full width; scroll up and down. Side clicks still turn pages; Space scrolls, then turns at the bottom
S or Shift pairingFix split two-page art in this issue only
F or FullscreenFill the whole screen
Mouse to top or bottom edgeShow the controls
Click the middle, or near the top or bottom edgeShow or hide the controls
HShow or hide this list

Shelf

The wallA section per publisher, every series a stack. The cover on the front is the next one to read; read issues are bagged and go to the back. Click a publisher's plate for its own rack
Click a stackFlip through it with the mouse wheel, a drag, or the arrow keys. Click the front copy (or Enter) to pull it out, then Read. Esc puts it back, Esc again goes back to the wall. All issues shows the whole series on a rack
/ or the search boxFind series and issues by title. Esc clears it
••• on an issue (or long-press it)Mark read or unread, use as the series cover
Long-press a series stackMark the whole series read or unread
Your placeOne spot per series. Opening a different issue in that series moves the spot there; half-read issues you leave go back to unread, finished ones stay finished
Back, or BackspaceBack one step: up a folder, out of a search, or out of the reader

OneDrive

Your libraryThe Publisher and Series folders in OneDrive\Apps\Panel Reader\Comics, the only folder Panel Reader can see. Issues stream as you read
ProgressSaved on the device and synced through OneDrive, so every device you sign in on picks up where you left off
