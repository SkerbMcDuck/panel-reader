Shelf

The wallThe main rack: a section per publisher, every series a stack. The cover on top is the next one to read; read issues are bagged and boarded at the back. Hover a stack and the top copy pokes up. Click a publisher label for its grid
Click a stackZooms in on it. Thumb through with the mouse wheel, a drag, or the arrow keys. Click the front copy (or Enter) to pull it out, then Read. Esc puts it back, Esc again goes back to the wall. All issues shows the series as a grid
Bagged and boardedRead issues, everywhere. Unread ones show the bare cover
J or Jump back inThe last 3 books you were reading (or what's up next)
/ or the search boxFind series and issues by title. Esc clears it
SortOrder the issues you're looking at by issue, writer, artist or cover date (writer, artist and date need tagged comics)
••• on an issueMark read or unread, use as series cover, add to a playlist
••• on a seriesMark the whole series read or unread, add it to a playlist
PlaylistsTop of the main shelf. Your own reading order across any series; Next issue follows the playlist. ••• inside one moves or removes issues
Wheel / Grid buttonCover wheel: spin it with the mouse wheel, ← → or a drag; click the middle cover (or Enter) to open it
Your placeOne spot per series. Opening a different issue in that series moves the spot there; half-read issues you leave go back to unread, finished ones stay finished
Back, Backspace, or your mouse's back buttonBack one step: up a folder, out of a search, or out of the reader

OneDrive and offline reading

Sign in to OneDriveShows the Publisher and Series folders in OneDrive\Apps\Panel Reader\Comics, the only folder Panel Reader can see. Issues stream as you read
••• then Keep on this deviceSaves an issue (or a whole series) for reading offline
••• then Remove from this deviceFrees up space. The OneDrive copy is untouched
Progress and playlistsSaved on the device and synced through OneDrive, so every device you sign in on picks up where you left off
