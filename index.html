Zoom

Pinch (touch)Zoom in and out
Double-tap (touch)Zoom in on that spot, double-tap again to zoom out
Ctrl + scroll wheel, or trackpad pinchZoom where the mouse is
+ / - / 0Zoom in / zoom out / back to full page
Drag, or scroll, while zoomedMove around the page
Swipe past the page's edge while zoomedTurns the page. Taps on the sides don't, so you can't flip away from a panel by accident
Turning the pageGoes back to full page

Issues

Next page on the last pageAlso opens the next issue

View

W or Fit widthOne page across the full width; scroll up and down. Side taps and left/right swipes still turn pages; Space scrolls, then turns at the bottom. Pinch or double-tap still zooms in further
S or Shift pairingFix split two-page art in this issue only
Full screenOn iPad it's automatic: opening an issue hides the time and battery bar, going back to the shelf brings it back. On PC use F or the Fullscreen button

OneDrive and offline reading

Connect OneDriveShows the Publisher and Series folders in OneDrive\Apps\Panel Reader\Comics, the only folder Panel Reader can see. Issues stream as you read
••• then Keep on this deviceSaves an issue (or a whole series) for reading offline
••• then Remove from this deviceFrees up space. The OneDrive copy is untouched
Import comicsAdds CBZ files from the Files app straight to this device
ProgressSaved on the device and synced through OneDrive, so every device you sign in on picks up where you left off
